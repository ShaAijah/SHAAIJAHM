About

Sha'Aijah McLaughlin is a graphic designer and visual artist based in Richmond, Virginia. Her work explores identity, memory, visual culture, and digital perception through typography, collage, publication design, and experimental image-making. Drawing from contemporary art, internet aesthetics, and editorial design, she creates work that balances conceptual research with bold visual experimentation. Whether designing books, posters, or identities, each project investigates how graphic design can communicate emotion, cultural narratives, and lived experience.
